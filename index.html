02 / PROFILE

Curious by nature.
Technical by practice.

I’m Daniel Delgado, a computer scientist and UX researcher interested in how people communicate, collaborate, and make sense of interactive systems.

I earned my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Florida in 2025. My research examined shared-gaze visualizations and collaboration in augmented reality, combining interaction design, experimental methods, prototyping, and quantitative analysis.

My work spans research and implementation: from studying user needs and evaluating experiences to building prototypes and investigating the systems behind them.
